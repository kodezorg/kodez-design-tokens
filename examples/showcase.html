Typography & Text
--kz-text-primaryHeadings · body Highest emphasis. Page titles, headings, primary content. Light: #231F20 (brand Light Black).
The quick brown fox Body copy — readable line height at 1.6.
--kz-text-secondaryLabels · support Supporting content: labels, secondary information, card descriptions. Dark: 7.0:1 on S0 → 5.4:1 on S5, all AA. Light: 4.5:1+ AA. Secondary paragraph — slightly muted for supporting copy and supplementary information.
--kz-text-tertiaryMetadata · hints Low-emphasis content: metadata, timestamps, helper text, placeholder-adjacent labels. Min AA at 4.5:1.
